## TREND 1: Reasoning Models & The Death of "Fast Answers" (0:45 - 2:30)

**[HOST]**

Okay, trend number one. And this one's subtle but huge.

**[TEXT: TREND #1 - REASONING MODELS]**

Remember GPT-4? You'd ask it a question, it would start typing immediately. Token by token. Fast, but sometimes... wrong. Especially on math, logic, multi-step problems.

The first answer was the ONLY answer.

**[DIAGRAM: Old models = immediate output]**

Now? Models like OpenAI's o-series, DeepSeek-R1, and Gemini 3? They THINK first.

**[ANIMATION: Model generating internal reasoning steps before final answer]**

They spend compute time working through the problem. Generating intermediate steps. Checking their work. THEN they give you the answer.

**[HOST]**

It's the difference between blurting out the first thing that comes to mind and actually... thinking.

**[B-ROLL: Researcher at whiteboard, equations, model architecture diagrams]**

The breakthrough here is something called RLVR - Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards.

Basically, instead of humans rating every response, the model practices problems with OBJECTIVELY VERIFIABLE answers. Math. Code. Logic puzzles. It gets rewarded when it's right. And it learns to think before it speaks.

**[HOST ON CAMERA]**

What to watch in 2026: Adaptive reasoning. Models that adjust their thinking time based on problem difficulty. Simple greeting? Fast response. Complex coding problem? Let me work through this.

This changes EVERYTHING for AI agents...
